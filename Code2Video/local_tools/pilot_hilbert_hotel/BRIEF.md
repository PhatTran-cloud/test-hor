# Hilbert's Infinite Hotel

This pilot turns Hilbert's Hotel into a visual system instead of a literal hotel drawing.

- Scene 1 uses a receding corridor and a waiting guest to make “full” a spatial question.
- Scene 2 makes the bijection `n -> n + 1` visible as a synchronized address wave.
- Scene 3 uses an even/odd zipper to absorb one infinite bus.
- Scene 4 uses unique factorization `r = 2^b(2s+1)` as a schematic map from bus-seat pairs to rooms.
- Scene 5 contrasts finite blockage with countable matching.
- Scene 6 lets the hotel respond to the guest as a living address machine.

The infinite hotel is a mathematical thought experiment. The room geometry is schematic, while the address rules are the authoritative mathematical model. The delivery mix is narration-only: no music bed and no SFX. Storyboard cue fields remain available as visual timing metadata for future revisions.
